What's going on?

The signal is only ever fully ON or fully OFF — but it switches so fast that your eyes only see the average. More ON time = brighter and faster.

Imagine flicking a water faucet all the way on and all the way off, over and over, really fast. If it's on half the time, you still fill the bucket — just half as quickly. PWM does the same thing with electricity.
